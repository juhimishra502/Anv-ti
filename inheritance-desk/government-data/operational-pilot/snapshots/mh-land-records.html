About the department

About the Department

Survey branch is one of the important branches in the office of Settlement Commissioner and Director of Land Records (Maharashtra State) Pune . This branch regularly monitors, the progress of survey work being carried out in the State. Various types of measurements such as Boundary Confirmation, Sub Division, Non Agriculture, Land Acquisition, Court Commission etc. are carried out by the Department staff working at Taluka level. District Superintendent of Land Records and Deputy Director of Land Records monitor the progress of this work in their District and Division respectively. Various steps are being taken for the modernization of Survey techniques in the Department. Survey branch also works on policy level changes in Survey and related aspects and proposes to the Government.

District Superintendent of Land Records, Mumbai Suburban District

The City Survey work in Mumbai Suburban District was carried out during the period 1963-1970. Mumbai Suburban District comprises a total of 86 villages and the City Survey records of all 86 villages are preserved and maintained through the Land Records Offices. For all the villages, separate property holder-wise property records and maps have been prepared in 1:500 scale.

This mainly involves various types of property/Land measurement (boundary demarcation, Sub-division, Non -Agriculture land, Land Acquisition, Road Setback, etc.), as well as recording and updating the records of property changes (Purchase Conveyance, Gift Deed, Release Deed,Mortgage deed,Deed of Assignment,Will/Legal Hiership,etc.).

The District Superintendent of Land Records, Mumbai Suburban District has 10 City Survey Offices and 1 Deputy Superintendent of Land Records Office under its Jurisdiction and works as the Controlling Officer over them. Also, the District Superintendent of Land Records is the technical advisor to the District Collector and the Appellate Officer of theCity Survey Officers.
