Surviving Members Certificate
Q. Where do I have to go and during which hours?
To the office of Sub-Divisional Magistrate in whose jurisdiction  in whose jurisdiction you reside during 9.30 a.m. to 6.00 p.m. on any working day.
Click Here
to obtain the contact address of the SDM in whose jurisdiction in your colony lies.
Q. Which papers/documents/fees, do I take with me?
Application on plain paper.
Affidavit to the following effects:- Details of deceased person i.e. name, age, address. Details of surviving members of deceased, their ages and relationship with the deceased. Purpose for which the certificate is required. For proforma of affidavit,
Click Here
Death Certificate.
Photocopy of ration card/any other proof of residence in Delhi.
Copy of letter/reference of the authority before whom the surviving members certificate is required (if any).
Q. What will be the criteria used while deciding my case?
After receiving the application, verification of the facts is carried out through the Tehsildar. On the basis of the report of Tehsildar & documents submitted by the applicant, the certificate is issued to the applicant.
Q. When will I get a response?
You should be getting a response normally within 21 days.
